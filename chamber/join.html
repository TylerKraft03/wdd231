NP Membership Benefits

Free membership for nonprofit organizations.

NP members receive:

  • Zero membership fee
  • Community and volunteer program promotion
  • Access to nonprofit events and workshops
  • Inclusion in our nonprofit spotlight directory

Bronze Membership Benefits

Great for smaller businesses getting started in the community.

Bronze members receive:

  • Access to monthly networking events
  • Chamber email newsletter promotion
  • Listing in the local business directory
  • Discounts on training and small advertising packages

Silver Membership Benefits

Perfect for growing businesses seeking stronger exposure.

Silver members receive:

  • Featured business listing and directory priority
  • Special pricing for event sponsorship and trade show booths
  • Invitations to member-only training sessions
  • Advertising highlights in newsletters and social media

Gold Membership Benefits

Best for businesses looking for maximum impact and VIP presence.

Gold members receive:

  • Homepage spotlight promotions
  • VIP access to major chamber events
  • Dedicated advertising opportunities and featured stories
  • Priority invitations to leadership roundtables and training sessions
